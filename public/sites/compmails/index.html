What you get, for nothing

    
A throwaway inbox that behaves like a real one while it lasts.

    

      
An address in a second
No account, no password, no CAPTCHA. A random address is generated for you and remembered in this browser, so a reload brings it back.

      
Live, automatic refresh
The inbox checks itself every few seconds and pauses while the tab is in the background, so a waiting verification mail appears without you touching anything.

      
Search and unread filter
Sorted newest first with a search box and an unread-only switch, which is the difference between a usable inbox and a wall of mail on a busy address.

      
Real messages, not a summary
HTML bodies render in a sandboxed frame, plain-text mail stays readable, and the raw headers are there when you need to see where something really came from.

      
Attachments and downloads
Attachments download straight from the reader, and any message can be saved as a .eml file or copied out as JSON.

      
Keeps your real inbox clean
Signups that would otherwise mean years of newsletters go to an address that stops existing instead. Your own inbox never learns about them.

Free temp mail questions

    

      
Is CompMails really free?
Yes — 100% free with no sign up, no trial and no paid tier. The inbox is created the moment the page loads, and the ads on the page are what pay for it.

      
Does a temporary address need a password?
No. A random inbox is created for you and remembered in your own browser, so there is nothing to register. If you want the same address back later, use Custom address and choose a password you will remember.

      
How long does a temp mail address last?
Inboxes are disposable and meant for short use: expect one to stay alive for a few days. The mail provider removes the messages afterwards, so treat everything here as temporary and never use it for an account you care about.

      
Can I receive verification links and attachments?
Yes. Verification links open straight from the message, HTML is rendered in a sandboxed frame, and attachments download from the toolbar in the reader.

      
Is a temp inbox private?
Treat it as public: anyone who knows the address can read it. Nothing is stored on our servers — the address and its session live in your browser and the mail is read through the mail provider's API — but it is still the wrong place for anything sensitive.

      
Can I pick my own temp mail address?
Yes. Custom address lets you choose the name, one of the provider's domains and a password, so you can log back into the same inbox later.
