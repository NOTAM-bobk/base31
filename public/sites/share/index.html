    How it works

    Three steps from file to link. Your files go straight from your browser to the host you choose, never through Relay.

    1. 01

      Drop your file

      Drag it in, tap to browse, or paste from your clipboard. Add as many files as you like.

    2. 02

      Pick an expiry

      Let the link last from one hour to thirty days, or keep it until you delete it.

    3. 03

      Share the link

      Copy it, scan the QR code, or use your device's share sheet. Anyone with the link can download.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Relay free to use?
    Yes. Relay is free and needs no account. Your files are stored by the hosting provider you choose (Gofile by default), and Relay itself never stores them.
    Do I need to sign up?
    No. Uploading with Gofile needs no account or API key. If you switch to file.io in the settings, you will need a free file.io account to get an API key.
    How big can my files be?
    Limits are set by the host, not by Relay. Gofile does not advertise a per-file size limit for guest uploads, and file.io limits depend on your plan. Very large uploads also depend on your connection and browser.
    How long does a link last?
    You choose: one hour, one day, seven days, thirty days, or never. Hosts may still remove files that go unused, according to their own policies.
    Is it private and secure?
    Uploads use HTTPS and links are unlisted, but anyone who has a link can open the file and files are not end-to-end encrypted. Avoid uploading sensitive information and use an expiry time.
    Can I delete a file after sharing it?
    Yes. Use the trash icon next to the file in your upload list. This works in the same browser you uploaded from, because the delete token is saved there.
    Does Relay keep a copy of my files?
    No. Files go straight from your browser to the host you selected. Relay's servers never receive them.

    1. What happens when you upload

    When you add a file, your browser sends it directly to the hosting provider you selected (Gofile by default, or file.io). Read the host's policy before uploading: Gofile and file.io.

    2. What Relay stores in your browser

    This data is not sent to us. Tokens and API keys act like passwords, so treat your browser profile accordingly. You can remove the list with "Clear list", delete a file from the list, or clear this site's data in your browser settings at any time.

    Like any website, the server that delivers this page may keep standard logs (such as IP address, browser type and request time) for security and to keep the site running. We do not use these logs to profile you.

    Relay sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. The page contacts a third party only when you upload, delete or expire a file, and only the host you selected.

    6. Retention and deletion
