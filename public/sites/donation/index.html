A few things to know.

Where does my contribution go?

Support helps with the project’s operating costs and continued development. The FundRazr campaign is the place to check its current details and updates.

Do I need to donate to use the directory?

No. Support is optional and doesn't buy a ranking, a guaranteed feature, or priority access. Explore, share a useful link, or report a bug—those help, too.

Who handles the payment?

The campaign is hosted on FundRazr. We don't collect payment details on this page. Review the campaign and the provider’s fees, payment options, and policies before contributing.

Can I ask a question first?

Of course. Email hello@base31.org and tell us what’s on your mind.
