1. Use of Temporary Email Service

                
This service provides disposable, temporary email addresses powered by the Mail.tm public API. Addresses are designed strictly for short-term testing, verification, and privacy preservation.

                
2. Data Retention & Expiration

                
Emails received on temporary addresses are automatically deleted after a retention period managed by Mail.tm (typically 7 days). Do not use this service for crucial personal or legal correspondence.

                
3. Acceptable Use

                
Users agree not to utilize this tool for fraudulent activity, spam dissemination, or unlawful purposes. Abuse may result in IP rate limiting or domain blocks.

            


            
            

                
Privacy Commitment

                
We prioritize your anonymity. We do not track personal identifiers, require sign-ups, or store logs of email contents on our frontend servers.

                
Third-Party Processing

                
All inbox requests, message fetching, and token generation are routed directly to the public REST endpoints of Mail.tm. Session data (JWT tokens and active mailbox credentials) is stored locally in your browser session storage.
