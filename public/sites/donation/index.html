Privacy Policy

Effective Date: Today.

Listen, we are developers building tools for the open web. We don't want to track you. We don't want your data. We just want to build cool stuff.

1. Data Collection

We collect exactly zero personal data unless you explicitly give it to us (like your email if you use a contact form or subscribe to a tier). If you donate via Stripe, they handle the payment data. We never see your credit card number.

2. Analytics

We might use a privacy-respecting analytics tool (like Plausible or basic Vercel analytics) just to see if anyone is actually visiting base31.org, but it's completely anonymized. No cookies required.

3. Third Parties

We don't sell data. Period. The only third parties involved are hosting providers (Vercel) and payment processors (Stripe/GitHub Sponsors).

Terms of Service

By using base31.org or donating, you agree to these extremely simple terms.

Rule #1: Be Cool

Don't use our tools for malicious purposes, spamming, or anything illegal. The open web relies on people being decent to each other.

Donations are Final

Donations are given freely to support development. They are non-refundable unless there was a massive technical glitch on our end. Think of it like buying a coffee—you can't return the coffee once it's brewed.

Uptime Guarantees

We try our best, but this is an indie project. Sometimes things break. We offer no strict SLAs or uptime guarantees. Use tools.base31.org at your own risk (though we try to keep it safe and stable!).

Cookie Policy

We prefer real chocolate chip cookies.

This site does not use any intrusive tracking cookies. The only cookies that might be set are essential ones from our payment providers (like Stripe) if you initiate a donation, to ensure the transaction goes through securely.

We don't use marketing pixels, retargeting cookies, or anything creepy. Enjoy your ad-free browsing experience.
